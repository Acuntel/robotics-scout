# The Rubric: What "Best" Means

Goal: the company most likely to capture durable value from the humanoid/robotics ramp, buyable by a US retail investor, at a price that does not already assume the ramp.

Each company scores 0-10 on each criterion. Composite = weighted average (out of 10). Weights are fixed for the full 30 days.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What 10 looks like | What 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Insourcing-proof** | 20% | Physically impossible for robot OEMs to bring in-house (e.g., mining rare earths) | Tesla/Figure/Unitree already make it internally |
| 2 | **Price-war-proof** | 15% | Policy moat, export controls, decade-long process secrets, or Western-only qualification protect margins from Chinese capacity floods | Commodity part; Hengli/Zhenyu-class capacity is coming online at 50% lower price |
| 3 | **Architecture-agnostic** | 15% | Needed in every robot regardless of design (rotary vs linear, any OEM winner) | Bet on one specific joint architecture or one OEM winning |
| 4 | **Real dollars today** | 15% | Disclosed, growing robotics revenue/orders in filings | Pure narrative; "samples only, no volume" |
| 5 | **Valuation gap** | 15% | Market prices it as a boring industrial; robotics is free optionality | 100x forward earnings or 100x sales; ramp fully priced (HDS, Leaderdrive) |
| 6 | **Content per robot × attach probability** | 10% | High dollar content AND high probability of being the supplier | Pennies per robot, or high content but likely loses the socket |
| 7 | **US accessibility** | 5% | NYSE/NASDAQ common stock | STAR Market / private / thin ADR |
| 8 | **Balance sheet & execution** | 5% | Net cash, clean execution record | Overlevered, missing own targets, key-person risk |

## Scoring discipline

- A score changes ONLY when new evidence lands. Every change gets a row in the SCOREBOARD changelog: date, old→new, one-line evidence with source.
- Guard against narrative drift: criterion 4 counts disclosed dollars, not TAM slides or analyst notes. Criterion 5 is measured against forward multiples, not vibes.
- The rubric encodes two hard-won lessons from the seed research: (a) the customer eats the component margin wherever it can (insourcing), and (b) the "Micron of robotics" framing cuts both ways — Micron's history is commodity price wars, which is exactly what Chinese screw/reducer capacity does to criterion 2.

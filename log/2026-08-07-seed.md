## Supply-chain structure (Aug 2026)

- **No single "NVIDIA of robotics" exists in hardware** because (a) a humanoid is ~40 actuators built from separate component industries with no universal substrate and no software lock-in, and (b) actuators are 60-70% of BOM, so OEMs insource: Tesla designs actuators in-house (Chinese suppliers ~70% of parts), Figure builds nearly everything at BotQ (targeting full China decoupling by summer 2026; >9,000 actuators produced), Unitree buys only 14-18% of parts cost externally.
- **The moat retreats down the stack**: robot → actuator → screw/gear → grinding machines. Planetary roller screws: ~14 per Optimus at $1,350-2,700 each (Morgan Stanley), ~3µm tolerances, ~12 qualified suppliers globally, thread-grinder backlogs reported ~18 months (market-research sourcing, unconfirmed by journalism). Grinder makers: Drake (Ohio), PTG Holroyd (UK), EMAG (WPG 7), Reishauer — all effectively private.
- **Bottleneck may invert**: Chinese screw capacity announced/built — Hengli 2.6M sets/yr (built, ramping 2026), Zhenyu 7.5M + 1M actuator modules (building), Xinjian ~1M (IPO filed). Chinese screws ~50% below Rollvis pricing. If humanoid volumes disappoint while this lands → price war, which kills criterion-2 scores for screw/gear makers.
- **Magnets**: ~1.3 kg NdPr magnet material per humanoid (a separate source says 3.5 kg total NdFeB for Optimus — different measures, don't mix). China export controls since Apr 2025, tightened early 2026. S&P (Jan 2026): ~9,000t NdPr deficit in 2026 (~10% of demand).
- **Already ran**: Harmonic Drive +150% 1yr, ~100x forward; Leaderdrive +152% 1yr, ~100x sales, ~450x trailing; Chinese chain (Sanhua, Tuopu, Shuanghuan) had its 2025 mania, now 30-45% off highs. "Hasn't run up" is only true of US-listed diversified industrials.
- **Order reality check**: RRX booked only ~$1M humanoid orders in Q1 2026 (vs $40M year-ago); RBC Bearings: "samples only, no volume" (Q4 FY26 call, May 2026). Narrative is years ahead of invoices.
- **Volume tell**: Tesla targeting 50k-150k Optimus units 2026, BOM $43k → <$20k target, Model S line converting to Optimus production. This single variable drives the whole component thesis.
- **Brain layer**: NVIDIA (Jetson Thor GA since Aug 2025; Isaac GR00T open humanoid reference on Unitree H2 Plus, Jun 2026) is default ex-Tesla (in-house AI5) and Figure (own compute). 2026 challengers: Qualcomm Dragonwing IQ10, AMD (Foundation Phantom MK-2), Google Gemini Robotics 2 On-Device. Model layer fragmenting; silicon lead intact.
